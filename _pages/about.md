I am a PhD student at EPFL in the IdePHICS lab, advised by Florent Krzakala. My research lies at the intersection of statistical physics, high-dimensional inference, and machine learning.

I am broadly interested in how structure can be recovered, learned, or exploited from noisy high-dimensional data — from matrix-tensor inference and approximate message passing to spectral methods, polynomial estimators, and learning dynamics.
